# Research Methodology

## Project
Pakistani Social Media Content Detective

## Research question
What types of social-media content are associated with higher engagement
among Pakistani audiences?

## Research status
This repository currently contains a starter dataset whose provenance and
representativeness have not been established. The starter dataset has no
confirmed Pakistan-labelled sample in the existing audit. Therefore, its
results must not be presented as evidence about Pakistani audiences.

## Planned research design
1. Identify a permitted, documented source of public social-media data.
2. Define the target population, collection period, platform, and inclusion
   criteria before collecting data.
3. Record the source, retrieval date, relevant API terms, fields collected,
   and any sampling limitations.
4. Remove duplicates and assess missing, invalid, and inconsistent values.
5. Define content categories before comparing their engagement.
6. Report likes, shares/reposts, and comments separately where comparable.
7. Consider follower count and platform differences when interpreting results.
8. Report sample size, distributions, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Operational definitions
- Engagement measures: the available likes, shares/reposts, and comments.
- Content type: a category defined in a documented coding scheme.
- Pakistani audience: must be supported by a defensible sampling or audience
  definition; a country field alone is not sufficient without documentation.

## Analysis plan
- Describe the sample and missingness.
- Compare engagement across documented content categories.
- Report medians and distributions alongside means because engagement may be
  skewed.
- Where appropriate, normalize or model engagement with audience size and
  platform differences taken into account.
- Avoid causal claims from observational data.

## Ethics and data handling
Use only sources and access methods permitted by the relevant terms and law.
Collect the minimum necessary data, protect personal information, and avoid
publishing sensitive or identifying content unnecessarily. API availability
does not automatically grant permission to reuse or redistribute data.

## Limitations
The current starter dataset's source, sampling procedure, and relationship to
Pakistani audiences have not been established. Findings from it are exploratory
software checks only, not conclusions about Pakistan.
